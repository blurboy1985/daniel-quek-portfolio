Haze helper · Website

Haze GoWhere

Tell it where you're going in Singapore, and it finds the route that keeps you indoors the longest, using trains, underground stations and covered walkways, based on live NEA haze readings.

More details
  • Shows which parts of the trip are indoors, sheltered or out in the open.
  • The worse the haze, the harder it avoids walking outside.
  • When the haze is really bad, it also suggests a taxi.

AI for statistics · Websites

SSIC & SSOC Decision Trails

Two sister apps that find Singapore's official codes for you. Describe a business to get its SSIC industry code, or paste a job ad to get its SSOC occupation code. You watch the AI choose, step by step, as a pixel-art crowd.

How it works, and how I check it
  1. Your text
  2. →
  3. Five rounds
  4. →
  5. Final five
  6. →
  7. Code
How it decides
It narrows the official list one level at a time using SingStat's own definitions, then picks from the best five. It shows how sure it is at every step.
How I check it
I compare its answers with the codes on real MyCareersFuture listings. Industry codes matched 10 of 12 companies. Job codes matched 4 of 10 ads, and most misses were ads that could fit more than one code.
