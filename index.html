Food · Website and iPhone app

Eat Simi

Scan a menu. Get dishes picked for you.

  • Picks for your group and budget, and skips dishes that mention your allergies.
  • In the app, it adds your picks to the restaurant's own order for you.
  • It never pays, and nothing is ordered until you say yes.
